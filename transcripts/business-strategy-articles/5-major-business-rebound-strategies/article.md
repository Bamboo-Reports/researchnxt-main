**5 MAJOR BUSINESS REBOUND STRATEGIES** — For the Southeast Asian Market in the New Normal

- **Customer-centric market.** Adoption of innovative and customer-centric operating models for capturing the growing market opportunities.
- **Omni storefront approach.** Maintaining an equivalent offline and online storefront.
- **Heightened focus on security.** Overhaul of Network security and privacy regulation to adapt to the new normal.
- **Rise of remote work force.** Rise Global Talent Accessibility and better collaboration.
- **Data-driven CX.** Data-Driven Organizations will have a clear edge.
