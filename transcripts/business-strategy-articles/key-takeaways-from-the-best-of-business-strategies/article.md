**Top 5 Takeaways from THE BEST OF BUSINESS STRATEGIES FOR SOUTHEAST ASIA IN THE NEW NORMAL GUIDE**

One of the things that the pandemic fast-forwarded in the business context is how we operated during and after recuperation. Beyond the initial knee-jerk impact, many short and long-term opportunities emerged and started reshaping the various business sectors to combat the unforeseen adversities of the impending crisis and prepare themselves to survive, thrive, and emerge with effective bounce back strategies.

- **AI & Digital Transformation.** Mobile-first, Digital-First & Fast Approach. Mounting Demand for Digital capabilities. AI powered Consumer Engagements.
- **Remote & Virtual Work Force.** Universal Acceptance of Remote Work. Evolved Remote Work Technologies. Transformation of Data and Network Security Operations. Rising Demand for Digital-first Roles.
- **Service Delivery Innovations.** Transact from Anywhere and across devices. Portfolio Diversification. Product/Service overhaul leveraging Tech.
- **Redesign of Supply Chains.** Strengthening of Final-mile Deliveries. Rise of Local-commerce. Emerging Supply Chain Models.
- **Data Driven Marketing.** Data-driven understanding of Customer Journeys. Focus on Localized Personalization. Level playing field for Small and Local Businesses.
