## Article

Today, generative artificial intelligence (Gen AI) is more than just a buzzword. It’s a transformative force reshaping how businesses operate. Yet, for many leaders, implementing AI for business effectively remains a significant challenge.

As the impact of AI continues to shape innovation, understanding its progression and potential has never been more critical for businesses.

That’s where Research NXT’s latest whitepaper, **“The Four Waves of AI,”** comes in as an essential resource for understanding (and eventually harnessing) AI’s potential.

**About the Whitepaper**

This guide, sponsored by Salesforce, offers a unique perspective by breaking down AI’s evolution into four distinct waves, making it easier for business leaders to understand where the technology’s origins lie. In our view, this discussion is an imperative to determine where this technology is headed.

From the early days of predictive analytics to today’s autonomous agents (and looking ahead to the potential of general artificial intelligence), the whitepaper provides a clear roadmap for business transformation.

**What You’ll Find:**

- A detailed breakdown of AI’s four transformative waves

- Predictions on how General AI will revolutionise industries

- A glossary of essential AI terms and concepts to simplify adoption

Whether you’re in sales, operations, or customer success, you’ll find relevant information tailored to your domain.

**How It Fits into the Bigger Picture**

*“**The Four Waves of AI”***serves as a prelude to our upcoming comprehensive report,**“Implementer’s Guide to AI.”**This report will provide a strategic framework, best practices, and real-world case studies for adopting AI across business functions.

As businesses stand at the crossroads of AI transformation, this whitepaper is your stepping stone to understanding the possibilities and preparing your organisation for a shared AI-driven future.

By breaking down complex concepts into digestible bits and providing a clear framework for implementation, we have created a practical toolkit for business leaders like you.

The whitepaper is available for download now, and readers can also pre-register for the upcoming “Implementer’s Guide to AI” report to receive early access to even more detailed insights and implementation strategies.

Download **“The Four Waves of AI”** and take the first step toward building a future-ready, AI-powered organisation.
