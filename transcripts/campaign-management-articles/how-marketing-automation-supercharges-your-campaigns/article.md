## Data, Data Everywhere: How it is Transforming Marketing

**“*In this day and age, marketing professionals and functional leaders will need to analyze various data points to determine which databases to measure and which metrics to focus on. This information is crucial for optimizing campaigns, improving team performance, and making informed investment decisions.*”** says Naresh Kumar, Member Leadership Staff at Zoho and a functional marketer in the SaaS industry, during an interview with Research NXT.

Data is the marketing elixir of 2024. By understanding your audience’s preferences and behaviours, you can craft campaigns that resonate with them. In the past, marketers may have attempted targeted campaigns, hoping their message would land with someone. But today, data allows for much more precise targeting.

Data helps to target the right people with the right message at the right time. By analyzing customer demographics, purchase history, and online behaviour, marketers can create profiles of their ideal customers. This empowers them to deliver targeted content that resonates with specific audiences, increasing engagement and conversion rates.

For instance, a travel company can track website visitors who browse specific vacation destinations. They can then serve targeted ads showcasing deals and promotions for those destinations while the customer is actively considering their travel options. This data-driven approach keeps your brand at the forefront of the customer’s mind, maximizing the potential for conversion.

## Decoding Data and Budget Challenges in Campaign Management

**“*One of the major issues marketers face while implementing marketing automation is managing scattered data across multiple platforms.*”** answers Naresh, when asked what challenges functional marketing experts face in executing multichannel campaigns.

Campaign managers struggle with data analytics due to an abundance of often unorganized information. Separating valuable insights from irrelevant data requires technical skills they may lack, and fragmented data across various platforms makes it difficult to see the bigger picture. This data overload can hinder their ability to make informed decisions and optimize campaigns.

Another challenge for marketers is securing adequate campaign budgets for H1 and H2 planning. Proving the ROI of marketing campaigns remains a challenge for functional experts.
